Where you'll be

The Area: Comasina

A quiet residential neighbourhood in the north of Milan, with fast, direct connections to the city centre, the railway and the motorway.

Getting here and getting around

  • 🚇 M3 metro (yellow line), Comasina stop — a 300 m walk. Direct to the Duomo with no changes (about 20 minutes).
  • 🚆 Milano Affori FN station (Trenord) — about a 10-minute walk: direct trains to Milano Cadorna and, changing at Bovisa, to Malpensa Airport.
  • 🏟️ Fiera Milano Rho — about 15–20 minutes by car, traffic permitting.
  • 🚗 A4 motorway, Cormano exit — about 1 km away. Convenient if you arrive by car.
  • 🏙️ Milano Centrale station — about 15 minutes on the M3, no changes. Bicocca — about 15 minutes by car.
  • ✈️ Linate Airport — about 25 minutes by car. Malpensa Airport — about 35 minutes by car.

Why Comasina is convenient

It isn't the historic centre — and that's exactly why it works for guests who want peace and quiet without giving up good connections.

🌳

Quiet neighbourhood

A mainly residential area, ideal for resting well between days out in the city.

🏙️

City centre by metro

The M3 takes you to the Duomo and central Milan with no changes, on a direct and predictable route.

🅿️

Easy by car too
