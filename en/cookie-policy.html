Cookie Policy

This page describes the cookies used by blueskymilan.it and how to manage your preferences. The Italian version is the legally binding text.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that the websites you visit send to your device, where they are stored and then sent back to the same websites on your next visit.

Technical cookies

The website only uses one technical item stored in your browser (localStorage) to remember the choice you made in the cookie banner, so that you are not asked again on every visit. It does not require prior consent because it is needed for the preference itself to work.

Cookie-free visitor statistics (Umami)

For visitor statistics we use Umami Cloud (umami.is), which sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device (neither cookies nor localStorage). Umami does not store your IP address and only produces anonymous, aggregated data, such as the number of visits, pages viewed, referring websites and clicks on the main buttons. None of the data you enter in the contact form is sent to Umami. Data is kept for a maximum of 6 months. For this reason your consent is not required and the banner does not apply to Umami. More details in the Privacy Policy.

Third-party content: Google Maps

A Google Maps map is available on the Home, "The Area" and "Contact" pages. The map is loaded only if you accept cookies in the banner or click "Show the map": only then does your browser connect to Google, which may set its own cookies under its privacy policy. Until you give consent you only see the address and a link to open the location in Google Maps in a new tab.

More information: Google Privacy Policy.

How to manage your preferences

You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browsing data for this website, or through your browser's cookie settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings.
