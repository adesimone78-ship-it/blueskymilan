The rooms

The Apartment

A self-contained 50 m² one-bedroom apartment, cared for down to the details and designed to make you feel at home from the first minute — on your own, as a couple or with up to 3 guests.

The rooms in detail

Bedroom

160x190 cm double bed, TV and a spacious wardrobe. A bright, quiet room designed for a good night's sleep after a day in the city or at work.

Living room

120x190 cm sofa bed and Smart TV — works both as a living area and as a second sleeping space, for up to 3 guests in total.

Kitchen

Fully equipped: induction hob, dishwasher, fridge, coffee machine, pots and pans, crockery and dining table. Food is not provided, but the supermarket is just a few steps away. Ideal if you are staying several days and prefer not to eat out every meal.

Private terrace

An outdoor space reserved just for you — rare in a city apartment and perfect for a coffee in the open air, an aperitivo or enjoying the sunsets over Milan (the photo shows the view from the terrace).

Bathroom

A full bathroom with bathtub, decorative tiles and plenty of natural light from the window.
