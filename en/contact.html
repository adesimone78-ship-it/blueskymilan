Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions. For anything else, message us on WhatsApp or by email.

How many guests can stay?

The apartment sleeps up to 3: a 160×190 double bed in the bedroom and a 120×190 sofa bed in the living room.

How does check-in work?

Check-in is fully self-service: you'll receive the instructions and access code in good time. Check-in is from 4 pm, check-out by 10 am.

Is there parking nearby?

The A4 Cormano motorway junction is about 1 km away. A private garage in the same building is available on request at €15 per day, to be booked in advance; otherwise there is pay-and-display parking (blue lines) in the nearby streets (Mon–Sat 8 am–7 pm).

Are pets allowed?

Please get in touch so we can discuss your request: we assess each case individually.

Can I book a long stay?

Yes, the apartment is designed for both short stays and longer ones, with dedicated rates. Write to us for a personalised quote.

How quickly do you reply?

We reply within a few hours, on WhatsApp and by email.
