Privacy Policy

Information provided under Articles 13 and 14 of EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) to users of this website and its contact services. The Italian version is the legally binding text.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Data controller

The data controller is the operator of Blue Sky Milano, Via Gerolamo Forni, Milan (MI), Italy — CIN IT015146C2O7BD2XEE.
Email: info@blueskymilan.it — WhatsApp: message us on WhatsApp.

What data we collect

Through the contact form and direct communications (email, WhatsApp) we collect the data you choose to give us: full name, email address, phone number, requested dates of stay and any information included in your message. We do not collect payment data through this website.

Purposes of processing

Your data is processed to answer requests for information and availability, to manage the booking of your stay and to comply with the legal obligations connected with accommodation (reporting guests to the police authorities, the guest register and the city tourist tax, where applicable).

Legal basis

Processing is based on pre-contractual or contractual measures requested by you (Art. 6.1.b GDPR) and on compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6.1.c GDPR).

Data retention

Data is kept for as long as necessary to handle your request or stay and, after that, for the period required by the tax and regulatory obligations that apply to accommodation businesses.

Disclosure

Your data is not sold or passed on to third parties for commercial purposes. It may be disclosed to third parties only to comply with legal obligations (e.g. public security authorities) or to provide the service (e.g. email service providers).

Your rights

At any time you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, or request data portability, by writing to info@blueskymilan.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it).

Visitor statistics (Umami)

To understand how the website is used (how many visits it receives, which websites or countries visitors come from, which pages are read and which buttons are clicked) we use Umami Cloud (umami.is), an analytics service that only collects anonymous, aggregated data.

Umami does not use cookies or any other identifier stored on your device, does not store your IP address and does not allow us to identify you or follow you across other websites. It records generic technical information such as the page visited, the referring website, country, device type, browser and operating system.

We also measure some actions on the website, for example clicks on the WhatsApp and email buttons or starting and sending the contact form. None of the data you enter in the form is sent to Umami: your name, email, phone number and message text are never transmitted. From the "dates of your stay" field we use at most the number of nights and the month of arrival, with no link to the person.

This processing is based on the data controller's legitimate interest in improving the website (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) and, because no cookies or data identifying the visitor are used, it does not require consent. Statistical data is kept for a maximum of 6 months.
